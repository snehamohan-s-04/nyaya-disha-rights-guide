# NyayaDisha – Legal Aid & Rights Awareness Portal

## About the Project

NyayaDisha is a Legal Aid & Rights Awareness Portal designed to help
citizens of India understand basic legal rights, find practical
information, and access trusted legal and government resources.

The project was developed as part of an academic community project.

## Objectives

- Increase awareness of basic legal rights
- Present legal information in simple language
- Help users identify appropriate next steps
- Provide important helpline information
- Connect users with official legal and government resources
- Support English and Kannada users
- Provide an accessible and mobile-friendly interface

## Main Sections

- Home
- Know Your Rights
- Legal Help
- FAQs
- Helplines

## Key Features

- English and Kannada language support
- Legal topic search and filtering
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Important helpline information
- Official government/legal resources
- Responsive design
- Senior-friendly interface
- Accessible navigation

## Disclaimer

This portal provides general legal awareness information for
educational purposes. It does not provide legal advice and should
not be considered a substitute for professional legal consultation.

## Academic Project

This website was developed as part of a college community project
focused on legal awareness and access to information.
